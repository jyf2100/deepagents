# Mercy Ledger: 狗子的戏剧人生

## Mercy Engine Overview

The protagonist shows mercy four times. Each costs something. Each pays off unexpectedly.

---

## Mercy 1: The Seed

**Movement:** II
**Recipient:** Minor enemy

**The Merciful Act:** Sparring life

**Immediate Cost:** Strategic disadvantage

**Later Payoff (Movement IV):** Unexpected aid

---

## Mercy 2: The Investment

**Movement:** III
**Recipient:** Innocent bystander

**The Merciful Act:** Protection

**Immediate Cost:** Energy and time

**Later Payoff (Movement V):** Information

---

## Mercy 3: The Risk

**Movement:** IV
**Recipient:** The companion 小明

**The Merciful Act:** Forgiveness

**Immediate Cost:** Emotional pain

**Later Payoff (Movement V):** Bond strengthened

---

## Mercy 4: The Impossible Gift

**Movement:** V
**Recipient:** The antagonist 暗影

**The Merciful Act:** Offer of redemption

**Immediate Cost:** Risk of defeat

**Later Payoff (Movement V (Beat 31)):** Antagonist's choice

---

## The Unexpected Agents (Beat 31)

At the moment of three failures, those who received mercy act to enable victory.
